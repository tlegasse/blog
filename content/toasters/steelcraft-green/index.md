This Steelcraft may well be museum quality, the usual condition you see is like [my red bodied Steelcraft](/toasters/steelcraft-red. A good tell is the shape of the micah. In the case of this example, it shows very little corrosion/carbon. The paint on the frame shows very sparse scuffs/ damage, the inner element cartridge frame wire shows very little corrosion, and the handles/ feet even still have all of their original paint. I consider this toaster to be among the most shining example of my collection.

Little is known about Steelcraft, but they sure have a lot of unique character. If you look from the side, you can see how the frame is slightly off square, with either side being parallel. This is an artifact of manufacturing, and I believe these were hand built. Incredible!
